About Eravathody

Founded in 1941 in the village of Thiruvilwamala, Kerala, the Eravathody Handloom Weavers’ Co-operative Society sustains a generations-old weaving tradition.

Rooted in the Mudaliar community’s belief in Deiva Thozhil — weaving as a sacred duty — the society continues to preserve authentic handloom craftsmanship through collective effort and cultural integrity.
