Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer international shipping?

+

Currently, our primary focus is on domestic orders within India. However, for bulk orders or special requests, please contact us directly, and we will try our best to accommodate international shipping.

Can we visit the handloom society?

+

Yes, we welcome visitors! We believe in transparency and love showing our craft to those interested. Please reach out to us via the contact form to schedule a visit so we can arrange a guided tour of the weaving process.

How long does it take to weave a Kasavu Saree?

+

A traditional Kasavu Saree takes anywhere from 5 to 10 days to weave, depending on the intricacy of the motif and the border thickness. Our master weavers ensure every thread is perfectly aligned for the highest quality finish.

How should I care for my handloom garments?

+

Handloom fabrics, especially those with pure Kasavu (zari), require gentle care. We recommend dry cleaning for the first few washes. If washing at home, use mild shampoo or soap nut, wash separately in cold water, and dry in the shade to preserve the colors and the golden zari.

Can I place a custom or bulk order?

+

Absolutely. We accept custom orders for sarees, dhotis, and yardage. For institutional or bulk requirements, please contact us directly at eravathody@gmail.com or call 04884 282 038 and we will work with you to meet your needs.
