What is Kasavu?

The term Kasavu does not refer to the garment — it refers to the zari, the golden thread woven into the borders. At Eravathody, gold-painted polyester zari yarn sourced from Surat is used as an authentic and affordable alternative to silver-wrapped silk zari. Every product bearing Kasavu has been handcrafted on pit looms and frame looms at the Eravathody Handloom Co-operative Society.
