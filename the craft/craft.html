The Art of Handloom

From Thread to Tradition

Each fabric is born from a meticulous process refined over generations.

The Journey of a Handloom Fabric

What appears as a seamless piece of fabric is actually the culmination of multiple intricate stages — each requiring specialized knowledge, precision, and years of experience. At Eravathody, we preserve these traditional techniques in their purest form.

The Weaving Journey

Follow the intricate stages from raw yarn to the final Kasavu masterpiece.

01

Winding

The journey begins with winding raw yarn from large hanks onto smaller bobbins or spools. This ensures uniform tension and makes the yarn manageable for subsequent processes.

02

Warping

Hundreds of threads are arranged parallel to one another on a large warping frame. This creates the warp — the lengthwise threads that form the foundation of the fabric.

03

Sizing

The warp threads are coated with a starch-based solution to strengthen them and reduce friction during weaving, protecting delicate threads from breaking under the stress of the loom.

04

Denting

Each warp thread is carefully drawn through the fine teeth of a metal comb-like structure called the reed. This determines the density and spacing of the fabric.

05

Drafting

The warp threads are then threaded through the heddles on the loom's shafts. This drafting process determines the weave pattern, dictating how the fabric will be structured.

06

Weaving

The weaver sits at the loom, using foot pedals to raise and lower the warp threads while passing the weft through with a shuttle. The rhythm of the loom creates the fabric inch by inch.

07

Finishing

Once the fabric is removed from the loom, it undergoes finishing processes such as washing to remove the sizing, ironing for smoothness, and inspection for any flaws.

More Than a Process

For our weavers, this is not merely a technical procedure. It is Deiva Thozhil — a divine responsibility passed down through generations. Each step is performed with reverence, and every fabric that emerges from our looms is a testament to the artisan's dedication.

In an age of mechanization, Eravathody continues to honor the slow, deliberate pace of handloom weaving. We believe that true craftsmanship cannot be rushed, and that the beauty of our fabrics lies in the human touch woven into every thread.
