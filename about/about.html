Sustaining Tradition Since 1941

Our Story

A journey through generations of handloom weaving in the heart of Kerala.

The Beginning

In 1941, amid the independence movement and a revival of indigenous crafts, a group of dedicated artisans in Thiruvilwamala came together to form the Eravathody Handloom Weavers' Co-operative Society.

The society was born from a desire to preserve the traditional weaving practices passed down through generations of the Mudaliar community — a community for whom weaving was not merely an occupation, but Deiva Thozhil, a sacred calling.

Thiruvilwamala, 1941

Our Mission & Values

Preservation

We protect and sustain the authentic handloom weaving techniques that have been refined over centuries, ensuring they are not lost to industrialization.

Community

Our co-operative model ensures fair wages, collective decision-making, and shared prosperity among all our weavers and their families.

Craftsmanship

Every fabric that leaves our looms carries the mark of skilled hands and patient dedication — a testament to quality over mass production.

Heritage

We honor Deiva Thozhil, the belief that weaving is a divine responsibility, connecting us to our ancestors and to the cultural fabric of Kerala.

A Living Legacy

Over eight decades, Eravathody has weathered economic shifts, technological changes, and evolving market demands. Yet, we remain steadfast in our commitment to handloom weaving.

Today, the society supports over 50 active weavers and their families, maintaining a balance between tradition and sustainability. Our fabrics — from traditional kasavu mundu to modern sarees — are recognized for their quality and authenticity across Kerala and beyond.

We continue to train younger generations, passing on not just technical skills but the philosophy that weaving is an art form deserving of respect, patience, and devotion.
